# Plugging niramay.me into health and wellbeing campaigns

Research date: 20 October 2026. Dates marked "TBC" follow each campaign's usual pattern but haven't been announced yet. Check them on the official page before committing to a date.

## 1. The one-line strategy

Niramay is small and local, so it can't outshout these campaigns. It can **show up inside them**. Each campaign below already has an audience, a hashtag, an event map and press coverage. If Niramay registers a small, well-themed offer in each one (a free online session, a blog post, a short), the campaign's channels do the distribution for us.

Niramay's edge is that it is **bilingual (DE/EN) and Indian-rooted**, has an online series that is running now, and covers *mind + body + community* (yoga, breath, dance therapy, kids, chair yoga). That fits the 2026 campaign themes almost exactly: loneliness, mental health at work, stress, and moving more.

### A structural lever: § 20 SGB V (health-insurance-subsidised courses)

Prevention courses certified by the **Zentrale Prüfstelle Prävention (ZPP)** are partly refunded by most statutory insurers (amount and cap vary by insurer, usually with 80 % attendance). Live-online courses can be certified too; the criteria were simplified in 2023. Certification is free and takes about 10 days.

- **Why it matters:** "Your Krankenkasse pays" is the strongest conversion argument in Germany. Insurers also list certified courses in their own course finders, which gives free visibility.
- **Fit:** *Yoga für Stress, Immunität & Schlaf* (8 sessions) is close to the standard format, which is 8–12 units in the "Stressbewältigung" or "Bewegung" field.
- **Caveat (to verify):** The ZPP requires a recognised **basic professional qualification** (e.g. sports, health or psychology degree or training) **plus** a qualifying yoga teacher training. Check Riju's and Richa's qualifications against the current *Leitfaden Prävention* before investing time. If they don't qualify, partner with a certified instructor or stay with the free-campaign route.

## 3. Content plan

### 3.1 Blog (bilingual; the site already has DE/EN slugs, categories and audio)

Write for **search terms people actually use** and for **what campaign organisers and journalists link to**. Each post links to one offer and one campaign.

| Campaign recaps | "So war unser Beitrag zur Woche der Seelischen Gesundheit" | Every campaign. Organisers often link to recaps |

Craft rules:
- One evidence-based statement per post, with a cited source (e.g. a study on yoga and stress or sleep). Describe *wellbeing* effects ("kann helfen, zu entspannen"), never cures. See Legal below.
- 900–1,400 words, a clear H2 structure, and an FAQ block for long-tail search.
- Every post ends with one clear call to action (free trial session, newsletter, or course) and gets cut into 3 shorts and 1 carousel.
- Guest posts and expert quotes: offer short expert pieces to local media (Esslinger Zeitung, Stuttgarter Zeitung lifestyle, Ostfildern Amtsblatt), expat media (IamExpat.de, The Local) and Indian diaspora portals around IDY.

## 5. Legal and trust guardrails (non-negotiable in Germany)

- **No healing promises (Heilmittelwerbegesetz § 3, UWG):** never say that yoga, Reiki, hypnosis, regression or astrology *cures*, *treats* or *heals* illnesses (depression, insomnia, anxiety disorders and so on). Talk about wellbeing, relaxation and self-care, and cite evidence for yoga or breathing claims. Reiki, past-life regression and astrology content should stay **out of health-campaign contexts** entirely, because it would undermine the credibility of the partnership and invites legal risk.
- **Heilpraktikergesetz:** without a Heilpraktiker licence, don't diagnose or treat illness. Add a short note to mental-health content: *"Kein Ersatz für ärztliche oder psychotherapeutische Behandlung."* Link to crisis resources (TelefonSeelsorge 0800 111 0 111 / 0800 111 0 222).
- **Campaign branding:** use campaign logos and hashtags only as each campaign's brand rules permit. Don't imply official endorsement by a ministry or embassy unless it is a formal partnership.
- **Image rights & privacy:** get written consent (KUG / GDPR) before filming participants, and **parental consent for children**, with blurring by default. Use an EU-hosted or privacy-reviewed video tool for sessions, tell participants if a session is recorded, and keep testimonials consented and genuine.
- **Music:** use licence-free tracks or platform libraries only. Commercial tracks in ads or YouTube uploads can trigger claims.
- **Verein vs freelancer:** free community events run under the **Verein** (until it is registered, never "e.V."). Paid services stay with Richa as a freelancer, as described in `src/legal/config.ts`.
